Why this matters

Investigators do not just read the narrative.
They study the thinking behind it.

Report writing as reasoning

When an officer writes an incident report, they are sequencing facts, assigning relevance, and explaining how events connect. That process reveals how they interpret events and what they believe the evidence supports.

Intent is traceable

The narrative can show motive, opportunity, means, and causal logic. Written records preserve the chain of reasoning from observation to conclusion, creating a reviewable record for legal and investigative assessment.

Text reveals pattern

By comparing verbs, rhetorical structure, temporal framing, and certainty markers, analysts can detect contradictions, behavioral drift, bias, or deliberate obfuscation.
