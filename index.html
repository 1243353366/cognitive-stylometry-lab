Why it matters

Writing is a record of how someone processed events.

The structure of a narrative tells you how a person organizes facts, explains causality, and handles uncertainty. Learn more than just what was written — learn how it was framed.

Intent becomes inferable through patterns such as purpose alignment, causal reasoning, detail selection, and narrative consistency. These are communication signals, not diagnoses.

Purpose alignment Clear / mixed / unclear
Causal reasoning High / moderate / weak
Detail selection Selective / balanced / sparse
Narrative consistency Stable / uneven / inconsistent
